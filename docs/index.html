1. Privacy Policy Overview

ShieldCall ("we", "us", "our") is designed from the ground up with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. We believe that your personal phone communications are private. Unlike traditional caller-ID apps that scrape your contact book or upload your private logs to third-party data brokers, ShieldCall performs all call screening locally on your hardware.

🔒 Zero Contact Book Scraping: ShieldCall does not require, request, or upload your personal contact list to any cloud server.

2. Android Telecom Permissions (Call Screening Role)

To protect you from robocalls, fraud centers, and unwanted telemarketers in real-time, ShieldCall requires the following Android permissions:

  • android.app.role.CALL_SCREENING (Call Screening Role): Enables the app's native background service to evaluate incoming phone numbers before your phone rings. Evaluation occurs against on-device Bloom filters, local prefix filters, and your personalized blocklists.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE: Allows the operating system to deliver incoming call state events to our local screening service.

How screening works: When a call arrives, Android passes the incoming phone number to SpamCallScreeningService. If the number matches an active quarantine rule or verified spam score threshold, the call is terminated silently without ringing. At no point is the audio of your call recorded, monitored, or accessible.

3. Data Collection & Google Play Data Safety

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, here is the complete summary of all data handled by ShieldCall:

  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to create a cloud account (via Google OAuth or email/password), we store your email address and display name for cross-device blocklist synchronization.
  • Blocklist & Ruleset Telemetry: Phone numbers and prefixes you explicitly add to your blocklist or quarantine list are synchronized with our encrypted cloud backend so your preferences carry over if you switch phones.
  • User-Submitted Spam Reports: When you voluntarily submit a spam report in the app, the target phone number, category (e.g., Robocall, Telemarketer, Scam), and optional descriptive note are added to our community threat database to protect other users.

Data Security: All communications between the ShieldCall mobile app and our backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

4. Emergency Calls Safeguard

⚠️ Important Notice Concerning Emergency Communications:
ShieldCall operates exclusively on incoming calls and will never intercept, restrict, delay, or interfere with outgoing or incoming calls to and from emergency numbers (such as 911, 112, 999, 199, or local emergency services).

6. Account & Data Deletion Policy

In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, you have the full right to delete your cloud account and all associated data at any time.

Method 1: In-App Self-Serve Deletion

Open the ShieldCall app -> navigate to Settings -> scroll to the bottom -> tap "Permanently Delete Account & Data". Confirm the prompt, and your account, credentials, and synchronized cloud blocklists are immediately and permanently erased.
